The emigrants arrived in Madinah with nothing. The Prophet ﷺ paired each one with a man of the city as a brother, with real obligations attached.

Sa'd ibn ar-Rabi' made his new brother Abdur-Rahman ibn Awf an offer that is hard to read without stopping: take half my wealth, and I will divorce one of my two wives so you can marry her.

Abdur-Rahman answered: may Allah bless you in your family and your wealth, just show me the market.

He went, he traded, and before long he was supporting himself. He died one of the wealthiest of the Companions.

Both halves of that exchange are the lesson. The offer was sincere, and the refusal was not ingratitude. Generosity that large was met by a man determined not to live on it a day longer than he had to.
