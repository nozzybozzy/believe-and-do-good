Ten thousand men entered Makkah without a battle. The city that had boycotted him in a valley for three years, tortured his companions, driven him out under a death sentence and fought him for eight years was now standing in the Haram waiting to hear what he would do.

He had entered with his head bowed so low over the saddle that his beard nearly touched it.

He asked them: *what do you think I am going to do with you?*

They said: a noble brother, and the son of a noble brother.

He gave them the words Yusuf gave the brothers who had thrown him into the well: no blame upon you this day. Go, you are free.

Twenty-one years of it, closed in a sentence. Hind, who had chewed Hamzah's liver at Uhud, accepted Islam. Wahshi, who killed him, accepted Islam.
