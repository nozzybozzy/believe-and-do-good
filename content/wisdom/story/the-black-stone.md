Quraysh rebuilt the Ka'bah and then nearly went to war over which clan would set the Black Stone back in the wall. It was the one honour nobody would concede, and men were preparing to fight over it inside the sanctuary.

They agreed to let the first man through the gate in the morning decide. It was Muhammad ﷺ, then about thirty-five, with no prophethood and no authority — only a reputation.

He asked for a sheet. He laid the stone in the middle of it and told each clan to take an edge. They carried it together to the wall, and then he lifted the stone himself and set it in place.

Everyone had carried it. Nobody had been excluded. Nobody had lost.

Most disputes that look like they need a winner need a sheet.
