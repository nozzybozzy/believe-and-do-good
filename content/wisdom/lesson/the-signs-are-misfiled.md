The *ayat* of creation are routinely read as proofs that God exists. But the Qur'an rarely argues with atheism, and when it deploys the signs it is almost always doing one of two other things.

It is establishing that **resurrection is not difficult** — look at the dead earth after rain, look at your own first creation from a drop, look at the fire you strike from a green tree.

Or it is establishing that **you have been given far more than you have noticed** — the water you drink, which you did not send down; the seed you sow, which you do not make grow.

Same verses. Different purpose. Read them as arguments for the Hereafter and for gratitude, and the whole class of passages reorganises itself.
