The first revealed word is **read**, and the last word of that same revealed passage is **prostrate**. Knowledge opens it; submission closes it.

Belief preceded law by thirteen years. Al-'Asr's three exceptions after faith are a sequence rather than a list — righteous deeds, then urging one another to truth, and then urging one another to patience, because patience is required *by* the urging. Ash-Sharh states its general rule only after establishing the personal record that proves it.

Wherever the Qur'an looks repetitive, check the ordering. That is usually where the argument is hiding.
