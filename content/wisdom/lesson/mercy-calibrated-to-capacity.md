*Fear Allah as much as you are able.* The obligation is explicitly sized to the person.

Al-Ma'un condemns those who are heedless **of** their prayer — not heedless *in* it. The distinction saves everyone who has ever lost concentration in a rak'ah.

Laylat al-Qadr is worth more than eighty-three years, and it is offered every year, to anyone.

And the last word of the last revealed surah is **Tawwaba** — the One who constantly accepts return. The Book closes its revelation not on triumph but on a door that stays open.
