Read across all 114, the Book keeps returning to one diagnosis and one remedy.

The diagnosis is that a human being, given everything, stops noticing, and a person who has stopped noticing will conclude he needs nothing, and a person who needs nothing will treat the powerless exactly as convenience dictates.

The remedy is not more information. It is remembrance, and the ground.

It is why the Qur'an spends so much of its length on gratitude and so little on proving God's existence. It is not arguing with someone who cannot see. It is arguing with someone who has stopped looking.
