It is a real blessing to be able to pray extra rak'ahs with ease, or finish the Qur'an more than once in the month. But it should not only be a matter of quantity — and of competition. It should be a matter of quality and reflection.

Rather than only reading in abundance, set time aside to genuinely reflect on the meaning of a short selection of ayat, and on the Sunnah teachings from Bukhari, Muslim and the rest. There are gems in every ayah. Take every ounce before moving to the next target.

**Set five goals, and review them.** We do this for work in annual targets and year-end reviews, and at school with progress reports and exams. Why not for the biggest exam, with the largest reward?

*Qur'an, Sunnah and prayer*

1. Memorise Surah Ya-Sin and understand its meaning.
2. Learn the ninety-nine names with their meanings, and invoke Him by them.
3. Pray on time — the Sunnah rak'ahs, Tarawih, Tahajjud. Build a real connection to the prayer, the first thing to be brought to account.
4. Make abundant dua, for yourself and for others. Learn the Prophets' duas.
5. Busy yourself with dhikr, and build the taqwa that shields you before you slip.

*Character and conduct*

1. Learn about rizq, and develop real tawakkul — actively seek your livelihood, and trust Him to deliver.
2. Want for your brothers and sisters what you want for yourself. Rid yourself of enmity, pride and jealousy.
3. Control your gaze — not only in public, but on your phone, in what you read, watch and listen to.
4. Control your anger. Help your family more. Be more grateful.
5. Cleanse the heart of hatred, of despair in difficulty, and of superficial wanting.

And the phrase to carry through it: **believe and do good deeds.** It occurs well over seventy times in the Qur'an — a coherent, insistent instruction said directly to us. *Those who believe and do righteous deeds — they are the companions of Paradise, abiding eternally therein* (2:82).
