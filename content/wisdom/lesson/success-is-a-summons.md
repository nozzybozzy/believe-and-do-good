An-Nasr announces the conquest of Makkah — twenty-one years of persecution ending in total victory — and the instruction that follows is **istighfar**. Ask forgiveness. The Prophet ﷺ read the surah as the announcement of his own death.

Ash-Sharh lists four completed favours — the chest expanded, the burden removed, the mention raised, and ease twice promised — and then says: *so when you have finished, stand up again.*

Relief is never presented in the Qur'an as a place to stop. It is presented as the moment you are now free to do the next thing.
