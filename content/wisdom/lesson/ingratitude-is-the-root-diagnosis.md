More often than the Qur'an calls a human being ignorant, it calls him **ungrateful**.

*Kanud* — counting the calamities and forgetting the favours. *Istighna'* — concluding that you need nothing, which is where Al-'Alaq locates transgression. *Takathur* — measuring yourself by what the next man has, until the graves settle the competition.

A great deal of what looks like theological error is diagnosed here as a failure of noticing. And that is a hopeful diagnosis, because noticing is trainable.
