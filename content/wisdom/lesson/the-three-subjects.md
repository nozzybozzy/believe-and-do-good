Any scheme can be imposed on the Qur'an from outside. A fine enough grid will catch every verse somewhere. But a grid that is merely fine is not the same as one that is true to the thing being sorted.

Ibn Kathir, explaining why the Prophet ﷺ called Surah Al-Ikhlas a third of the Qur'an, records the reasoning: the Qur'an has three subjects, and Al-Ikhlas is the whole of one of them.

| The three subjects | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Tawhid** | Who Allah is, His oneness, names, attributes, and sole right to be worshipped |
| **Al-Akhbar / Al-Qasas** | Reports, creation, history, the messengers, the nations, the unseen |
| **Al-Ahkam wa'l-Waʿd wa'l-Waʿid** | Commands and prohibitions, and the promise and the threat attached to them |

Everything else is an expansion of those three. Nothing is smuggled in.
