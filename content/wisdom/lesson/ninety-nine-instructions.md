Not themes or inferences, direct instructions, each with the ayah it comes from, so any of them can be checked.

Speech and temper, do not be rude in speech (3:159) · restrain anger (3:134) · speak mildly (20:44) · lower your voice (31:19) · do not ridicule others (49:11) · do not call one another by offensive nicknames (49:11) · speak justly (6:152) · say what is best (17:53) · do not utter a word of disrespect to parents (17:23)

Toward people, be good to others (4:36) · forgive people their mistakes (7:199) · do not be arrogant (7:13) · do not spy (49:12) · avoid suspicion (49:12) · do not backbite (49:12) · repel evil with what is better (41:34) · be just, even against yourself (4:135) · do not let hatred of a people make you unjust (5:8) · walk with humility (25:63)

**Family**, be dutiful to parents (17:23) · lower to them the wing of humility (17:24) · do not enter their private room without permission (24:58) · treat women kindly (4:19) · do not forget generosity between yourselves (2:237)

Wealth and dealings, write down the debt (2:282) · give the orphan his property (4:2) · do not consume wealth unjustly (2:188) · do not give short measure (83:1-3) · fulfil your promises (17:34) · give ease to one in difficulty (2:280) · do not follow what you do not know (17:36)

**Before Allah**, do not associate anything with Him (4:36) · do not despair of His mercy (39:53) · seek His help with patience and prayer (2:45) · do not lose hope (12:87) · remember Him often (33:41) · be grateful (2:152) · trust in Him (3:159) · ask Him for His bounty (4:32)

The full list runs to nearly a hundred. Its point is not its length. It is that almost every one of these is a matter of conduct toward another person, and each carries a reference, so none of it rests on anyone's opinion.
