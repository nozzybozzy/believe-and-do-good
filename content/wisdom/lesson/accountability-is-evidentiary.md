The earth is told to speak, and speaks. The limbs testify against their owner. What is in the chest is turned out and made known. The record is read, and then handed to you to read yourself.

Nothing in the Qur'an's Judgement is asserted without being demonstrated. The verdict always arrives with its evidence attached, and usually the evidence is produced by the accused.

That is an argument about the character of the Judge, not merely a description of a procedure. A power that could simply decree is instead shown taking the trouble to prove.
