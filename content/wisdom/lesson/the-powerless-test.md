Al-Ma'un asks whether you have seen the man who denies the Recompense — and then answers with behaviour rather than argument: *that is the one who shoves the orphan away.*

Al-Balad says man was created inside hardship, and that the only road out is uphill — and then defines the uphill road as relieving somebody else's hardship: freeing a neck, feeding on a day of hunger, an orphan of near relationship, a poor person in the dust.

The Qur'an's standard test of belief is behavioural, and it is deliberately aimed at people who cannot reciprocate. Anyone can be decent to someone who might be useful later. The test is set where there is no return.
