An entire class of revelation exists to steady the person under pressure.

*They said the same to messengers before you.* *Your Lord has not forsaken you, nor is He displeased.* *Proclaim it openly, and We will handle the mockers.*

Any framework that files these under prophetology loses the point. They are addressed to the Prophet ﷺ in the first instance and to the reader permanently. The Book assumes that carrying something true will cost you something, and it puts the consolation in the text in advance.
