Not a power, a suggestion. He withdraws at remembrance and returns at heedlessness, and the fact that he has been reduced to whispering is evidence of his defeat, not of your corruption.

The counter-measure the Book prescribes is almost embarrassingly small: dhikr, and two words, *I seek refuge*.

That proportion is itself the argument. If the threat required an equal and opposite force, the Qur'an would have prescribed one. It prescribes a sentence, because the threat is a sentence.
