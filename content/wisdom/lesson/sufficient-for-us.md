> **Hasbuna Allahu wa ni'ma-l-Wakil** — Sufficient for us is Allah, and He is the best Disposer of affairs. (3:173)

Ibn Abbas reported that Ibrahim said it when he was thrown into the fire. And the Prophet ﷺ and the believers said it when they were told an army had gathered against them — and it *increased them in faith* rather than fear.

The Prophet ﷺ also gave it to Companions who were shaken on hearing that the angel of the Trumpet already has it at his lips, waiting for the command.

Three situations with nothing in common except that in none of them was there anything left to do.

That is the point of the phrase. It is not a way of avoiding action — *and whoever relies upon Allah, He is sufficient for him* (65:3) comes in a passage about doing the right thing in a difficult matter. It is what you say once your own part is finished, and the rest was never yours.

Submitting the outcome is not a burden added. It is a weight put down.
