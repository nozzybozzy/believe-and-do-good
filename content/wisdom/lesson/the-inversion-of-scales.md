Birds with pebbles defeat elephants. The man called *cut off* is given abundance, and the insult is handed back to the one who threw it. The noblest lineage in Arabia shields nobody, Abu Lahab was the Prophet's own uncle. Fortresses fail and reliance holds.

The Qur'an does not merely say that worldly measures are unreliable. It takes them up one at a time, size, wealth, lineage, fortification, numbers, examines each, and reverses it in front of you.

And it does this most sharply at the moments when the worldly measure looked decisive. Hunayn, with twelve thousand men, is the case it names.
