> Know that the life of this world is only **play** and **amusement**, and **adornment**, and **mutual boasting** among you, and **rivalry in wealth and children**. (57:20)

Five words, and they are not a list. They are a sequence — and the sequence is your life, in order.

1. **Play** — *la'ib.* We start as toddlers, and play is the whole of what matters.
2. **Amusement** — *lahw.* A little older, and we need entertaining.
3. **Adornment** — *zinah.* The teens, obsessed with how things look.
4. **Boasting** — *tafakhur.* Independent, earning, and the urge to show it.
5. **Rivalry in wealth and children** — *takathur fi-l-amwal wa-l-awlad.* Married, settled, measuring against the neighbours.

In five words Allah has compressed the entire human thought process from cradle to old age, in the exact order a person passes through it.

Then the verse turns: rain that pleases the farmers, growth, yellowing, and then straw. And the verdict — *the life of this world is only a deceiving enjoyment.*

It does not tell you the five stages are evil. It tells you what they are worth when the rain stops.
