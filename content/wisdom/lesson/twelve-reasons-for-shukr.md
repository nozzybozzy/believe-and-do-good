1. **You could never count them.** *If you should count the favours of Allah, you could not enumerate them* (16:18).
2. **Making you ungrateful is the enemy's stated mission.** After refusing to bow, he said: *You will not find most of them grateful* (7:17). That is the objective, named at the outset.
3. **The opposite of shukr is kufr.** The Qur'an sets them against each other: *be grateful to Me, and do not deny Me* (2:152). Disbelief is, at root, ingratitude toward the One who gave you everything.
4. **The benefit is entirely yours.** *Whoever is grateful is grateful for his own good* (31:12). Ingratitude does not diminish Him.
5. **Worship without it does not hold.** *Be grateful to Allah, if it is Him you worship* (2:172).
6. **It earns His pleasure.** *If you are grateful, He is pleased with you* (39:7), and the Prophet ﷺ said Allah is pleased with a servant who eats and thanks Him for it, drinks and thanks Him for it.
7. **It is a stated purpose of your creation.** He gave hearing, sight and hearts *that you may be grateful* (16:78).
8. **Four things are the best of both worlds**, a grateful heart, a remembering tongue, a body that endures trial, and a faithful spouse.
9. **It has a twin.** *Wondrous is the affair of the believer: if good comes, he thanks, and that is good for him; if hardship comes, he is patient, and that is good for him.* Gratitude and patience between them cover every possible day.
10. **His bounty reaches everyone**, *most of the people are not grateful* (2:243) is said of people still being provided for.
11. **Most people thank Him without noticing.** *Thank God* falls out of the mouths of believer and atheist alike.
12. **It compounds.** *If you are grateful, I will surely increase you* (14:7). The only promise in the Qur'an framed as an unconditional multiplication.
