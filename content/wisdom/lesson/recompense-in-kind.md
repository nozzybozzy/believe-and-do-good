*Jazaʾ min jins al-ʿamal.* The punishment in the Qur'an is rarely just severe. It is the deed handed back in the shape it was done in.

Al-Humazah crushed people's reputations, and is thrown into the Crusher. He enclosed his wealth and counted it, and is enclosed himself with the gates shut over him. Abu Lahab's wife carried thorns to lay in the Prophet's path, and carries fuel. Quraysh were fed against hunger and made safe from fear, and An-Nahl 112 describes the same two gifts withdrawn as the exact two punishments.

Once you see it, you cannot unsee it, and it changes what a warning is. It is not an arbitrary threat attached to a rule. It is a description of what the act already is, played back at full length.
