> So glorify the praise of your Lord and ask His forgiveness. Indeed, He is ever accepting of repentance.

**Tawwaba.** The form is intensive: not one who forgave once, but one who keeps accepting return, repeatedly, as a standing disposition.

By the ordering of revelation, this is among the last of it. The Qur'an does not close on victory, or on law, or on judgement. It closes on a door that has been left open.
