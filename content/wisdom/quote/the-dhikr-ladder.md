Each phrase matched to the moment it answers, so that the remembrance is not generic, but the specific thing that state requires.

| When | Say |
|---|---|
| Always start with Allah, and good intentions | **Bismillah** |
| Seek refuge | **A'udhu billah** |
| Repent | **Astaghfirullah** |
| Be grateful and humble | **SubhanAllah** |
| Look how far you've come | **Alhamdulillah** |
| Zoom out | **La ilaha illa Allah · Allahu Akbar** |
| This too shall pass | **In sha' Allah** |
| The best is yet to come | **Masha Allah** |
| Pardoning and goodness | **'Afwa · 'Afiyah** |
| He decrees and does what He wills | **Qadar Allahu wa ma sha'a fa'al** |
| There is no power nor might except by Allah | **La hawla wa la quwwata illa billahi-l-'aliyyi-l-'azim** |
| Allah is enough for me | **Hasbiya Allah** |
| Allah is sufficient for us, the best Disposer of affairs | **Hasbuna Allahu wa ni'ma-l-Wakil** |

And the one that settles the whole question of contentment:

> Raditu billahi Rabban, wa bil-Islami dinan, wa bi Muhammadin ﷺ nabiyya
> I am pleased with Allah as my Lord, with Islam as my religion, and with Muhammad ﷺ as my Prophet.
