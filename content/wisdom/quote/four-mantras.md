Short enough to interrupt a spiral before it builds.

> **Hasbunallahu wa ni'ma-l-Wakil** — Sufficient for us is Allah, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.
> *For when it is entirely outside your control.*

> **This is for me, not to me.**
> *For turning a barrier into a lesson, or a protection, placed deliberately.*

> **What is meant for me will never miss me; what missed me was never meant for me.**
> *For killing regret over a job, an opportunity, a person.*

> **The pain is real, but the misery is optional.**
> *For letting the hurt be real without handing it the whole house.*

The second and third are the doctrine of *qadar* in everyday clothes: *no disaster strikes except that it is in a register before We bring it into being* (57:22).
