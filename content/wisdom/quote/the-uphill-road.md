> But he has not attempted the steep road. And what will make you know what the steep road is? It is the freeing of a neck, or feeding on a day of severe hunger — an orphan of near relationship, or a needy person in misery.

The surah has just said that the human being was created inside hardship. Then it asks why he has not taken the road out. And then it defines the road out as relieving somebody else's hardship.

The way out of your own difficulty is through someone else's.
