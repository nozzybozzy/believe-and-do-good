> Part of the excellence of a person's faith is leaving what does not concern him.

Set it beside 49:11–12 — do not mock one another, avoid excessive suspicion, do not spy, do not backbite — and beside the last verse of Al-Kafirun: *for you your way, and for me mine* (109:6).

A boundary, drawn after a clear statement of your own position. Live, then let live.
