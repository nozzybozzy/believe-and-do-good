> So woe to those who pray — those who are heedless **of** their prayer.

The preposition carries the whole verse. It does not say heedless *in* their prayer, which would condemn everyone who has ever lost their place in a rak'ah. It says heedless *of* it — those who let it go entirely, who pray it outside its time, who treat it as optional.

One letter, and the difference between a warning aimed at neglect and a warning aimed at human distraction.
