> I wish you enough **pain** to make you value the good times you have.
> I wish you enough **heartache** to honour others' hearts and safeguard your own.
> I wish you enough **loss** to teach you contentment with what you already have.
> I wish you enough **stress** to learn how to manage and focus your mind.
> I wish you enough **failure** to find the right path and come back fighting every time.
> I wish you enough **faith** to believe in a positive future that you *will* build.
> I wish you enough **lessons** to make an even wiser person out of you.
> And lastly, enough **hellos** to get you through the final goodbye.

Note what is being wished on you. Not the absence of hardship — *enough* of it. The quantity is the whole prayer, and it is the same measure the Qur'an sets: *Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity* (2:286).
