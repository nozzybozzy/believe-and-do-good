*The phrase this site is named for appears in the last stanza.*

> People are living much more on a lot less
> The more we greed and desire the more we stress,
> Enjoy the money you have, enjoy it now
> For it could all be gone, someday, somehow.
>
> Fortunes change hands, luck comes and goes
> It can damage one's character, history shows,
> Don't neglect those around you
> Who were there before and after.. And will still love you.
>
> Debt is a pain, it is a curse
> Don't fall into this trap, it makes matters worse,
> Seek to live life within one's means
> The happiest man could survive on toast and beans.
>
> It's humbling, it's raw, it has taught us a lesson
> Stick to your day job and find your real passion,
> Desire and greed must be tamed
> But Allah can forgive all, no need to seek blame.
>
> Your health and time is the real wealth
> Enjoy it with loved ones, before it has left,
> Forgive and forget, move on and build strong
> A strong foundation; a home, where you truly belong.
>
> Don't seek to avoid life's pains and troubles
> Create a life that you don't have to break from,
> Working together, to provide and support
> Nothing else is worth more effort.
>
> A mistake, it is just that, a minor blip in time
> But avoid hurting others' feelings, that is the true crime,
> **Believe and do good to all; black or white, rich or poor, young and old**
> And remember.. all that glitters is not gold.
