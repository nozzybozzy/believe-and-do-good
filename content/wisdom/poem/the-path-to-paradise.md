*Written in 2004. The closing line is where this site gets its name.*

> All the world's joys and all the world's toys
> Amount to nothing once you've heard His voice..
> What did you do with the life that I gave you?
> What did you send Me after all that I sent you?
> Did you send thanks?
> Did you send peace?
> Did you even pray?
> Or did you just feast..
> Feast for money, for food, for girls
> For clothes, for cars, and expensive pearls..
>
> They taught you good
> They taught you humility
> But yet you stand here
> Lost in front of me
> I sent you Prophets
> I sent you books
> I care not of your power
> Or good looks
>
> But you should have sent Me duas
> You should have sent cries
> I was there my slave
> Especially through the nights
>
> Instead..
> You cried over love
> You cried over loss
> You enjoyed making money
> And impressing your boss
>
> I sent you pain
> I sent you tests
> For you to learn
> I didn't love you any less
>
> All are equal, in My view
> But those who subdued, will outdo you
> For them await gardens
> Rivers and beautiful things
> No chance for you
> Now doesn't it sting..
>
> Life is but one chance
> No going back now
> If only you stopped and thought
> Why am I here, and how?
>
> The Path to Me is long and winding
> Taqwa and Iman you must guard
> Love one another, help each one through
> Be merciful to others, I shall show mercy to you
>
> Learn of the book (Qur'an), learn of the path (Islam)
> You will be strong in this life, with Me in your heart
> To Me you shall return...
>
> The gates of Heaven will be open to you
> **Just believe and do good**
> **Is what I tell you.**
