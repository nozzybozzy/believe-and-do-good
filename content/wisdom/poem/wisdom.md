*Written in 2004, and the reason the Wisdom section carries a question mark over its own title.*

> It's not called wisdom,
> and it's not the solution,
> it's about our lives,
> and the ways we can live them.
>
> Through time we see,
> and with age we learn,
> don't think to change the world,
> just with the money you earn.
>
> Mine isn't wisdom,
> neither is it new,
> from the Greatest did it spring,
> sent for me and you.
>
> So turn to that,
> which is always better,
> **just believe and do good,**
> it weighs heavily in the matter.
>
> Our end goal is to be with Him,
> our struggles and worldly aims,
> leading to all the wrong paths,
> when all along it was laid down before us,
> with a manual — the Qur'an,
> and a teacher too.
>
> So turn to it my friend,
> both me,
> and you.
