*Written in June 2020.*

> Instead of focusing
> On my skin colour,
> Please can we look
> One step further..
>
> When you cut me
> Do I not shed
> The same viscous liquid
> Of blood, that is red?
>
> When I cry
> Do my eyes not secrete
> Those transparent tears
> Of watery beads?
>
> I eat the same food
> I sleep in the same way
> I exercise and move
> I find similar sports to play
>
> I study and read
> The same books as you
> Learning, being inspired
> To grow and improve
>
> When I am ill
> I shake and develop a fever
> I worry and panic
> And visit the same doctor
>
> I take the same white pills
> And pay the same red bills
> I walk, run and drive like you
> Wearing sandals and trainers and shoes
>
> When I die and am buried
> And my flesh is gone
> I leave the same ivory white bones
> Like my teeth, but now shown
>
> We have more in common
> Than many try to suggest
> Find the inner beauty
> And ignore the rest.

The Qur'an settles it in one clause: *We made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another. Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous* (49:13). The differences are for recognition. The ranking is by something else entirely.
