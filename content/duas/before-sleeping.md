The Prophet ﷺ would say this on going to bed, and on waking would say *praise be to Allah who gave us life after He caused us to die, and to Him is the resurrection.*

The pairing is deliberate, and the Qur'an makes the same one: *Allah takes the souls at the time of their death, and those that have not died during their sleep* (39:42). Sleep is treated as a small death and waking as a small resurrection, practised nightly, so the large one is not a stranger.
