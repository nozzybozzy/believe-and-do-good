Reported with a striking list of what attaches to saying it: deeds recorded, sins removed, ranks raised, and protection from Shaytan for the rest of the day.

Read what it actually says. Four clauses, and not one of them is a request. It states who He is, that nothing stands beside Him, that the dominion and the praise are His, and that nothing is beyond His power.

The most rewarded sentences in Islam tend to be the ones that ask for nothing at all.
