Al-'Abbas asked the Prophet ﷺ to teach him a dua, and this is what he was given. He came back days later saying it seemed a little short — he wanted something bigger. The answer: *my dear uncle, ask Allah for 'afiyah, for by Allah you cannot be given anything better than it.*

*'Afiyah* has no single English word. Health is 'afiyah. Enough to live on is 'afiyah. Your children safe is 'afiyah. Being forgiven and not punished is 'afiyah. It covers this world and the next, and it is five words long.
