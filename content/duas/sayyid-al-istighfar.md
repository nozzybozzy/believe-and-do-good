The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever says this during the day with certainty in it and dies before evening is among the people of Paradise, and likewise whoever says it at night and dies before morning.

Read the clause that carries the whole thing: *as much as I am able.* The covenant is acknowledged and the shortfall is admitted in the same breath. And then the two acknowledgements are placed side by side, Your favour on me, and my sin, which is the exact posture the Qur'an keeps asking for.
