## Meaning

The One who gives each created thing its particular shape, the third of the three creation names, and the most specific. Not that a human being exists, but that *this* one has these features and no one else's.

## Lessons for the believer

*He formed you and perfected your forms* (40:64). *In whatever form He willed He assembled you* (82:8).

A great deal of modern unhappiness is a quarrel with this name, with a face, a height, a body, a limitation. The Qur'an does not respond by saying appearance does not matter. It says the form was chosen, deliberately, by the One who is never careless, and that the choosing was itself an act of shaping you for something.
