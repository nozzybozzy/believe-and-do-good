## Meaning

The same root as al-Ghaffār, covering, but a different intensity. Where al-Ghaffār stresses repetition, al-Ghafūr stresses completeness: the covering is total, and what is covered does not resurface.

## Lessons for the believer

The verse most often given to people in despair is built on it: *say, O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed Allah forgives all sins. Indeed it is He who is al-Ghafūr, ar-Raḥīm* (39:53).

Notice that it is addressed to people who have already transgressed, and that the prohibition in it is not against sinning, that has happened, but against despairing. Despair is treated as the more dangerous of the two.
