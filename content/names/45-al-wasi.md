## Meaning

Vastness — of knowledge, of mercy, of giving. The root also produces *wusʿ*, capacity: *Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity* (2:286).

## Lessons for the believer

Both uses matter. His capacity to give is limitless; your capacity to bear is known exactly and never exceeded.

People behave as though mercy were a finite resource being drawn down by everyone else's need, or by their own repeated use of it. *My mercy encompasses all things* (7:156). Nothing is being used up.
