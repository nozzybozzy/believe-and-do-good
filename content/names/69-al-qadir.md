## Meaning

The One with the power to do a thing — and from the same root, *qadar*, the measuring out of destiny. Power joined to exact measure, not raw force.

## Lessons for the believer

*Say: He is the One Able to send upon you punishment from above you or from beneath your feet* (6:65). And equally: able to relieve it.

The phrase repeated across the Qur'an is *ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr* — over every thing competent. Every thing. The size of what you are asking for is not a variable in the equation; it never was.
