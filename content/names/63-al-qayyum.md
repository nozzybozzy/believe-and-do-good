## Meaning

From *qāma*, to stand. Two claims in one word: He stands in need of nothing, and everything else stands only because He holds it up.

## Lessons for the believer

The verse continues: *neither drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep.* Not a poetic flourish, an argument. Anything that sleeps has to hand over what it was maintaining. He never hands over.

For a person who feels that everything depends on them, this is the correction. The universe is being held up, continuously, by someone else. Your part in it is smaller and safer than it feels at three in the morning.
