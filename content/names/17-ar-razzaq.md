## Meaning

*Rizq* is wider than income. It is anything that sustains you and that you benefit from: food, health, knowledge, a spouse, a friend, a child, an afternoon of calm.

## Lessons for the believer

*There is no creature on earth but that its provision is upon Allah* (11:6). Every one of them — including the ones nobody is feeding.

The Prophet ﷺ said that if you relied upon Allah as He should be relied upon, you would be given provision as the birds are: they leave hungry in the morning and return full. Note that the birds still fly out. Reliance was never a synonym for sitting still.
