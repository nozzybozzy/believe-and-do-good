## Meaning

A witness is one who is present and who can testify. Ash-Shahīd is present to everything, and His testimony is the final word on what happened.

## Lessons for the believer

At the Farewell Pilgrimage the Prophet ﷺ asked the crowd whether he had delivered the message, and when they said he had, he raised his finger and said: *O Allah, bear witness.*

That is the instinct this name produces. When a matter is genuinely between you and Him, whether an intention, a private wrong or an accusation you cannot answer, the case is already fully recorded by the only Witness who was actually there.
