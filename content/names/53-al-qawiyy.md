## Meaning

Strength without limit and without fatigue — strength that does not have to be conserved, recovered or rationed.

## Lessons for the believer

It is usually paired with al-ʿAzīz: *indeed Allah is Qawiyy, ʿAzīz* — strong and unassailable. Strength that can also never be overcome.

The Prophet ﷺ said the strong believer is better and more beloved to Allah than the weak believer, and there is good in both. Then, immediately: be eager for what benefits you, seek Allah's help, and do not give up. Strength is asked for from the One who has it in full, and then used.
