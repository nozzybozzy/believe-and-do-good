## Meaning

To overpower completely, so that the other has no move left. *Al-Wāḥid al-Qahhār*, the One, the Subduer, is how the Qur'an answers the question of whether anything could stand beside Him.

## Lessons for the believer

Its most frequent use is about death. Everyone who has ever thought themselves untouchable has been subdued by it, without exception and on schedule.

For someone under an oppression they cannot escape, this name is not abstract. The Qur'an's picture of the Day of Judgement has the people emerging and the question being asked, *whose is the sovereignty today?*, and the answer given, because every rival has already been overcome.
