## Meaning

Wisdom: putting each thing exactly where it belongs, at exactly the right time. The root also gives *ḥikmah*, and *iḥkām*, making a thing sound and firm.

## Lessons for the believer

It is the most common partner of al-ʿAzīz in the Qur'an, and that pairing is the answer to the oldest objection people raise about God. Power alone would be terrifying. Power exercised with perfect wisdom is something else.

The angels said it when they questioned the creation of a being who would spill blood: *exalted are You, we have no knowledge except what You taught us; indeed it is You who are al-ʿAlīm, al-Ḥakīm* (2:32). They did not receive an explanation. They received a correction about who holds the knowledge.
