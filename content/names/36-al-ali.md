## Meaning

Highness in every sense — of essence, of rank, of power. Above His creation, and above anything that could be said of Him falsely.

## Lessons for the believer

This is the name said in every sujūd: *subḥāna Rabbiya l-Aʿlā* — glory to my Lord, the Most High — spoken from the lowest position the body can take, with the face on the ground.

That is not a coincidence of liturgy. The Prophet ﷺ said the closest a servant is to his Lord is while in prostration. The name that puts the greatest distance between you and Him is said at the moment of least distance.
