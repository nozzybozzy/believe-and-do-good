## Meaning

The One who determines and brings into being. The root also gives *khuluq*, character, the shape a thing is made in, whether physical or moral.

## Lessons for the believer

Three names come together in the Qur'an, *al-Khāliq, al-Bāriʾ, al-Muṣawwir*, and they are stages rather than synonyms: the decree of a thing, its bringing into existence, and the giving of its particular form.

Nothing about you is accidental at any of those three stages. The Qur'an's favourite argument for the resurrection is built on this name: the One who made it the first time, out of nothing, is not going to find it difficult the second time, out of something.
