## Meaning

The One who takes retribution from those who persist in wrong after the truth has reached them. In the Qur'an the verbal form is always tied to the guilty and never arbitrary.

## Lessons for the believer

*Indeed, We will take retribution from the criminals* (32:22) — after the verse describes someone reminded of the signs of his Lord who then turned away from them. The condition is stated before the consequence.

For the wronged, this name is what makes it possible to stop. You do not have to carry the case yourself, and you do not have to become an oppressor to settle it. It is filed with someone who does not miss.
