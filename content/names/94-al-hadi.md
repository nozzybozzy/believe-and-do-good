## Meaning

The One who guides — both by showing the way, and by carrying the heart along it. The two are distinct, and the second belongs to Him alone.

## Lessons for the believer

*You do not guide whom you love, but Allah guides whom He wills* (28:56), revealed about the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Talib — the uncle who protected him for forty years and died outside the faith.

That verse is the cure for two diseases at once: despairing over someone you cannot bring, and taking credit for someone you did. Your job is the showing. The rest belongs to this name, which is why the most repeated request in Islam is *guide us*.
