## Meaning

To lower, to bring down. Its pair is Ar-Rāfiʿ, the One who raises. The Qur'an uses the verbal form of the Day of Judgement: *bringing down and raising up* (56:3), an event that rearranges every ranking that existed.

## Lessons for the believer

The same command is given to you in one of the gentlest verses in the Book: *lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy* (17:24), about parents. The root is the same.

He lowers the arrogant. You are asked to lower yourself willingly. One of those is much less painful than the other.
