## Meaning

The One who gathers, mankind on a Day about which there is no doubt (3:9), and hearts that had been divided.

## Lessons for the believer

It has a consoling use that people reach for at funerals and partings, and an alarming one about the Day of Gathering, and they are the same name.

Its social form is in 8:63: *He brought their hearts together. Had you spent all that is on the earth, you could not have brought their hearts together, but Allah brought them together.* Said of the Aws and the Khazraj, who had been killing each other for generations. Unity is described there as something that cannot be purchased, only given.
