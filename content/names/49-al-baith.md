## Meaning

To send forth and to raise up. The Qur'an uses the verb for both of the things this name covers: raising the dead from their graves, and sending prophets to people who had gone spiritually dead.

## Lessons for the believer

*That is because Allah is the Truth, and He gives life to the dead, and He is over all things competent; and the Hour is coming, no doubt about it, and Allah will resurrect those in the graves* (22:6–7).

The two senses are one argument. A people are described as dead before the messenger comes and alive afterwards. If He does that to a nation with a recitation, the grave is not a difficulty.
