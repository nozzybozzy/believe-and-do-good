## Meaning

Goodness that overflows — kindness given generously and without calculation. The same root gives *birr*, the dutiful goodness owed to parents, and the wider *birr* of 2:177, which the Qur'an defines not as facing a direction in prayer but as belief plus giving to the people who need it.

## Lessons for the believer

*Indeed, we used to call upon Him before. Indeed, it is He who is al-Barr, ar-Raḥīm* (52:28) — said by the people of Paradise, looking back.

The name is a standard as well as a description. A person who takes it in becomes easier to live with: *birr* is measured in how you treat your parents, your neighbour and the person who can do nothing for you.
