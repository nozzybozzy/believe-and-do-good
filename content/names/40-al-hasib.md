## Meaning

Two meanings from one root. The One who takes account — every deed weighed exactly. And the One who suffices — *ḥasbī Allāh*, Allah is enough for me.

## Lessons for the believer

The pairing is not accidental. The One who will settle every account is the reason you do not have to settle them all yourself.

*Sufficient is Allah as Reckoner* (4:6). Umar's advice follows from it: take account of yourselves before you are taken to account, and weigh your deeds before they are weighed for you.
