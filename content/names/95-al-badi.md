## Meaning

The One who originates with no model to copy. *Badīʿ as-samāwāti wa-l-arḍ* — Originator of the heavens and the earth (2:117) — and when He decrees a matter, He says to it *be*, and it is.

## Lessons for the believer

Everything a human being makes is a rearrangement of something that already existed. This name marks the one category of making that is not.

And it is the answer to an impossible request. When Maryam asked how she could have a child untouched, this is the name the answer runs through: He creates what He wills; when He decrees a thing, He says *be*.
