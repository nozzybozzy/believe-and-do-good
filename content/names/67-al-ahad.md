## Meaning

Close to al-Wāḥid but sharper. *Wāḥid* says there is no second. *Aḥad* says there are no parts, He cannot be divided, analysed into components, or added to.

## Lessons for the believer

Surah Al-Ikhlas answers a question about Allah's lineage by destroying the premise: *He is Allah, Aḥad*, nothing produced Him, nothing came out of Him, nothing stands beside Him for comparison.

And this is the word Bilal said on the burning sand with a rock on his chest, while being told to renounce or die. *Ahad. Ahad.* The most abstract statement in the Qur'an turns out to be the one a tortured man holds on to.
