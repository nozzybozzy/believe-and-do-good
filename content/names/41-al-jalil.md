## Meaning

*Jalāl* is majesty, greatness that inspires awe rather than affection. It is the counterweight to *jamāl*, beauty, and the tradition holds the two together deliberately.

## Lessons for the believer

The Qur'an closes Surah Ar-Rahman on this: *blessed is the name of your Lord, Owner of Majesty and Honour.*

A faith built only on majesty becomes fear. Built only on gentleness it becomes presumption. The names are given in both registers so that the believer walks between hope and fear, which is exactly how the classical scholars described the healthy heart.
