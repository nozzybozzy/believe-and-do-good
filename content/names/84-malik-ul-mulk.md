## Meaning

Not merely the King, but the owner of kingship itself — the One who hands it out and takes it back.

## Lessons for the believer

The dua of 3:26 is the fullest statement of it: *Say: O Allah, Owner of Sovereignty, You give sovereignty to whom You will and remove it from whom You will; You honour whom You will and humble whom You will. In Your hand is all good.*

Every ruler in history has held a loan. It is worth reciting when watching the news, and worth reciting when your own small authority — over a team, a household, a room — starts to feel like a possession.
