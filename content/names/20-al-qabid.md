## Meaning

To take hold of, to draw in, to restrict. It is always understood alongside its pair, al-Bāsiṭ, the One who expands — *Allah constricts and expands, and to Him you will be returned* (2:245).

## Lessons for the believer

The pairing is the whole teaching. Restriction is not the absence of God's action; it is God's action, from the same hand as the expansion.

Ease that never ended would make you forget. Difficulty that never lifted would break you. The alternation is deliberate, and it is administered by One who knows exactly how much you can hold — which is the argument of 2:286.
