## Meaning

The intensive of al-Qādir: power that is not merely possessed but fully and effectively exercised.

## Lessons for the believer

Surah Al-Qamar tells the stories of destroyed nations and closes on it: *indeed the righteous will be among gardens and rivers, in a seat of honour near a Sovereign, Muqtadir* (54:54–55).

The word that ended the accounts of Pharaoh and Thamud is the same word used for the King the righteous end up sitting near. The power is one power; where you stand in relation to it is what differs.
