## Meaning

Greatness that belongs to its owner by right. In a human being the same word is a vice, *takabbur*, arrogance, because a creature claiming greatness is claiming what is not his. In Allah it is simple accuracy.

## Lessons for the believer

This is the name that makes pride absurd rather than merely rude. The hadith qudsi has it starkly: greatness is His cloak and might His garment, and whoever contends with Him over either is punished.

Arrogance is not a bad manner. It is a small being trying on clothing that does not fit, in front of the One it belongs to. The cure is not to think less of yourself. It is to look at Him for a moment and get the proportions right.
