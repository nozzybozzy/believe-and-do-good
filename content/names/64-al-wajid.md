## Meaning

The One who finds whatever He seeks and never lacks what He needs. Nothing is beyond His reach and nothing is missing from His possession.

## Lessons for the believer

Human resourcefulness runs out. There is a point in most difficulties where a person has genuinely tried everything they can think of.

This name says that point is a limit of yours, not of His. The story of Hajar is its illustration: she ran seven times between two hills, which was everything available to her, and the water came up somewhere she had not been looking.
