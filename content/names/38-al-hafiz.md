## Meaning

To guard, preserve and keep from loss. It covers protection from harm and preservation from being forgotten, the same root as memorising the Qur'an, *ḥifẓ*.

## Lessons for the believer

The largest demonstration of this name is the Book itself: *indeed, We revealed the Reminder, and indeed, We are its Guardian* (15:9). Fourteen centuries, and a child in any country can recite it identically to a child in any other.

The Prophet ﷺ gave the personal form of it to Ibn Abbas as a boy: *be mindful of Allah, and He will protect you.* The protection is described as a response.
