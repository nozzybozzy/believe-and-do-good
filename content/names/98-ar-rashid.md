## Meaning

*Rushd* is right-mindedness, sound judgement that leads to a good end. Ar-Rashīd directs all affairs to their proper outcome, without error and without needing advice.

## Lessons for the believer

The young men of the cave asked precisely for this: *grant us from Yourself mercy and prepare for us from our affair rashadā* (18:10), right direction in a situation they could not see the end of.

It is the name behind *istikhārah*. You are not asking to be told the answer. You are asking the One whose management never errs to arrange the matter and settle your heart on it.
