## Meaning

*Majd* combines greatness with generosity — honour that gives rather than merely commands. It is used of the Qur'an itself: *Qāf, by the glorious Qur'an* (50:1).

## Lessons for the believer

It appears in the closing of the prayer every Muslim says in every salah, on Ibrahim and his family: *innaka Ḥamīdun Majīd* — indeed You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.

Sitting in that position, the believer is not asking for anything at all. Some of what is said to Him is simply description, offered because it is true.
