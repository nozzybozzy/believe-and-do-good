## Meaning

The One who makes others free of need — the active counterpart of al-Ghaniyy. He has no need, and He removes need from whom He wills.

## Lessons for the believer

*And that it is He who enriches and suffices* (53:48).

But the enrichment the Prophet ﷺ described is not an amount: *richness is not in having many possessions; richness is contentment of the soul.* Two people with identical bank balances can be in completely different conditions in front of this name, because what is being given is sufficiency, not a sum.
