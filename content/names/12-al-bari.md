## Meaning

To bring into existence, distinct and separate, with no defect. The same root gives *barāʾah*, being free or clear of something, and *bariʾa*, to recover from illness.

## Lessons for the believer

The name says that what He brings into being comes out clean. Whatever damage there is in a life arrived later.

That is worth holding when someone tells you they are beyond repair. The One who originated them free of flaw is the same One who is described as *al-Jabbār*, the mender. Origination is His speciality, and He does it more than once.
