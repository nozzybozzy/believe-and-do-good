## Meaning

*Fatḥ* is opening, and in Arabic it covers three things at once: opening what is closed, deciding what is disputed, and granting victory. The conquest of Makkah is a *fatḥ*. A judge's ruling is a *fatḥ*. The first surah of the Qur'an is *al-Fātiḥah*, the Opener.

## Lessons for the believer

*Whatever mercy Allah grants to people, none can withhold it; and whatever He withholds, none can release it afterwards* (35:2).

That verse is doing two jobs. It removes the fear that someone is standing between you and what is meant for you. And it removes the illusion that you can force a door that is closed. Both are relief, if you let them be.
