## Meaning

Truth and reality in one word, with a third sense underneath: a *ḥaqq* is also a right, something owed. He is the Real, as against everything whose existence is borrowed; the True, as against every claim; and the One to whom everything is owed.

## Lessons for the believer

*That is because Allah is al-Ḥaqq, and what they call upon besides Him is falsehood* (22:62).

Everything else in your field of vision is contingent — it began, it depends, it will end. Sorting the real from the apparent is most of what the Qur'an trains a person to do, and this name is the fixed point the sorting is done against.
