## Meaning

Hearing that misses nothing, not the spoken and not the unspoken, not the crowd and not the whisper.

## Lessons for the believer

A woman came to the Prophet ﷺ arguing about her husband, in a low voice, in a corner of the room. A'ishah said she was in the same house and could not make out the words. The opening of Surah Al-Mujadilah came down: *Allah has heard the words of the woman who disputes with you concerning her husband... and Allah hears your dialogue* (58:1).

The whole exchange was overheard from above the heavens. That is the standard this name sets: the complaint nobody would sit still for was answered in revelation.
