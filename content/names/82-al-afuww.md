## Meaning

*ʿAfw* goes further than forgiveness. *Maghfirah* covers a sin; *ʿafw* erases it, so that there is nothing left to be covered. The root also means to efface, as wind erases tracks in sand.

## Lessons for the believer

A'ishah asked the Prophet ﷺ what she should say if she found Laylat al-Qadr. He taught her: *O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love to pardon, so pardon me.*

Consider what is being asked for on the best night of the year — not wealth, not even Paradise directly, but erasure. And note the clause in the middle: *You love to pardon.* The asking is not an imposition. It is an invitation being taken up.
