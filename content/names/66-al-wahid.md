## Meaning

One, in the sense of unique and without partner, nothing beside Him, nothing like Him, nothing sharing in what He does.

## Lessons for the believer

It is the name that organises everything else. *Say: Allah is Creator of all things, and He is al-Wāḥid, al-Qahhār* (13:16).

Its practical form is sincerity. If He is one, worship cannot be split. That is why Surah Al-Kafirun refuses a compromise twice over in six verses: not because the compromise was unreasonable by Makkan standards, but because divided worship contradicts the fact this name states.
