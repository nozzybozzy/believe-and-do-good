## Meaning

Greatness in every respect, of being, of attribute, of authority, beyond what a mind can encompass.

## Lessons for the believer

It is the name a Muslim says in every rukūʿ of every prayer: *subḥāna Rabbiya l-ʿAẓīm.* Bowed at the waist, saying: my Lord is the Tremendous.

The posture and the sentence are one statement. And it sits at the end of Ayat al-Kursi, after the verse has expanded from His knowledge to His Kursi encompassing the heavens and the earth: *and He is al-ʿAlī, al-ʿAẓīm.*

Nothing is large in front of this name. That includes whatever you are carrying.
