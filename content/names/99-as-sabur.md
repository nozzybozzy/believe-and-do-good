## Meaning

Patience without limit. Close to al-Ḥalīm, with a difference of emphasis: forbearance is about withholding a deserved punishment, while *ṣabr* here is about the sheer duration — not being hurried, ever, by anything.

## Lessons for the believer

The Prophet ﷺ said no one is more patient with an offensive word he hears than Allah: people attribute a son to Him, and He still gives them health and provides for them.

Read that slowly. The patience is not distant tolerance. It is continuing to feed and heal people in the middle of their denial of Him.

And it is the last name on the list, which is fitting. Whatever you are waiting for Him to change in you, He is not impatient about it.
