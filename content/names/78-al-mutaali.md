## Meaning

The intensive of al-ʿAlī: exalted far above anything that could be attributed to Him, imagined of Him, or compared with Him.

## Lessons for the believer

*Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, the Grand, al-Mutaʿālī* (13:9).

It is the name that governs how a Muslim talks about God. Every anthropomorphism, every reduction of Him to a bigger version of us, every confident claim about why He did something, all of it runs into this name. The honest position in front of it is usually silence, and then *subḥānahu*.
