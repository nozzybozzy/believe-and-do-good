## Meaning

The One who brings life to its end, at an appointed time that does not move.

## Lessons for the believer

The pairing with al-Muḥyī is constant, and it is what keeps this name from being frightening in isolation. The One who ends life is the One who gives it, and who will give it again.

The Prophet ﷺ instructed frequent remembrance of *the destroyer of pleasures*. Not to make people morbid: to correct the estimate. Most bad decisions are made by someone who has quietly assumed there is more time than there is.
