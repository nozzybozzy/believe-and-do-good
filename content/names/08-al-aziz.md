## Meaning

The root means to be rare, precious and out of reach. *ʿIzzah* is honour that cannot be taken. Al-ʿAzīz is the One no force can overcome and no scheme can outmanoeuvre.

It is almost always paired in the Qur'an with another name — *al-ʿAzīz al-Ḥakīm*, *al-ʿAzīz ar-Raḥīm* — because unchecked power is not the point being made.

## Lessons for the believer

Look at the pairing. Might joined to wisdom, might joined to mercy. A power that could do anything, described as doing it wisely and compassionately.

And the corollary for you: *to Allah belongs all honour, and to His Messenger, and to the believers* (63:8). Honour is not something you extract from people's opinion of you. It is on loan from this name, and it is available in only one place.
