## Meaning

*Karam* is generosity joined to nobility. It is not merely giving much. It is giving without calculation, without humiliating the recipient, and without being asked.

## Lessons for the believer

The Qur'an uses it to confront the human being directly: *O mankind, what has deceived you concerning your Lord, al-Karīm?* (82:6), the very generosity that should have produced gratitude has instead been read as permission.

The Prophet ﷺ taught the dua of Laylat al-Qadr around a related word: *O Allah, You are pardoning and You love to pardon, so pardon me.* Generosity is described as something He *likes* to exercise. Asking is not an imposition on it.
