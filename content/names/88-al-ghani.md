## Meaning

Rich in the sense of needing nothing. Not wealthy by comparison, free of need altogether, which no created thing is.

## Lessons for the believer

*O mankind, you are those in need of Allah, and Allah is al-Ghaniyy, al-Ḥamīd* (35:15). The verse states the asymmetry plainly.

It also settles what worship is for. He gains nothing from your prayer and loses nothing by your neglect. In the hadith qudsi: if the first of you and the last of you, your humans and your jinn, were as pious as the most pious heart among you, it would not increase His kingdom in anything. The benefit runs one way, and it is not toward Him.
