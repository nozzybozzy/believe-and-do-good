## Meaning

A *waliyy* is near, the word carries closeness, alliance and responsibility together. It is the guardian who acts for you and the friend who takes your side.

## Lessons for the believer

*Allah is the waliyy of those who believe. He brings them out of darkness into light* (2:257).

The relationship is described as mutual. In the hadith qudsi on drawing near, Allah says He becomes the hearing with which His servant hears and the sight with which he sees, a description of how completely the *walī* of Allah is taken charge of.

And there is nothing to fear for them, *nor will they grieve* (10:62): not about the future, not about the past.
