## Meaning

Solid, firm, unyielding. Where al-Qawiyy is the magnitude of strength, al-Matīn is its permanence, strength that does not weaken over time.

## Lessons for the believer

*Indeed, Allah is ar-Razzāq, Owner of Power, al-Matīn* (51:58). Provision, power and firmness in one verse.

Most human failure is not a failure of intensity but of duration. People can be devout for a week. The Prophet ﷺ said the deeds most beloved to Allah are the most consistent, even if small, which is asking you for a small share of what this name has entirely.
