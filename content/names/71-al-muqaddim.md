## Meaning

The One who brings a thing forward, advances it, gives it precedence, in time, or in rank.

## Lessons for the believer

Its pair is al-Muʾakhkhir, the One who delays. The Prophet ﷺ used both together in a dua of forgiveness: forgive me what I have sent forward and what I have left behind, *You are al-Muqaddim and al-Muʾakhkhir.*

Everything arrives in an order, and the order is authored. Some things came to you early that you were not ready for. Some are being held back, and that too is an act rather than an absence.
