## Meaning

Utterly pure, free of any fault, any partner, any resemblance to the created. The root carries the sense of being set apart by purity, which is why the Holy Land is *al-arḍ al-muqaddasah* and the angel of revelation is *Rūḥ al-Qudus*.

## Lessons for the believer

Most of what people reject about God is really a projection: a being with human pettiness, human need, human vanity, scaled up. Al-Quddūs cuts that off. Whatever defect you imagine, He is free of it.

And the name sets a direction. He is pure, He loves purity, and the heart is the organ meant to be kept clean. Every act of repentance is an attempt to move toward this name.
