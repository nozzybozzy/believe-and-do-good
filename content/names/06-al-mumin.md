## Meaning

From the root of *amn*, security, and *īmān*, faith. Two readings, both sound: the One who gives security to His creation, and the One who confirms the truth of His messengers by the signs He sends with them.

## Lessons for the believer

Fear is the ordinary condition of anyone who looks at the world honestly. Things break; people leave; the future is not in your hands.

This name does not deny that. It relocates the safety. You are not secure because nothing can reach you; you are secure because the One from whom nothing is hidden has undertaken to be your protector. The same root produced *īmān* — which tells you that faith and safety are, in Arabic, the same idea.
