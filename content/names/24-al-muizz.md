## Meaning

The One who gives *ʿizzah*, honour, dignity, strength that cannot be stripped. It comes from the same root as al-ʿAzīz, and its pair is al-Mudhill.

## Lessons for the believer

The dua of 3:26 places both in a single sentence: *You honour whom You will and You humble whom You will; in Your hand is all good.*

Read that last clause carefully. It does not say honour is the good and humbling is the bad. It says all good is in His hand, including, sometimes, being brought low. Umar said it plainly: we were a people whom Allah honoured through Islam, and if we seek honour through anything else, He will humiliate us.
