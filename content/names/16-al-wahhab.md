## Meaning

*Hibah* is a gift, given freely, with no exchange, no debt discharged, no return expected. Al-Wahhāb gives in that mode, constantly.

## Lessons for the believer

Two duas in the Qur'an end on this name, and both ask for something unpurchasable. *Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us from Yourself mercy, indeed You are al-Wahhāb* (3:8). And Sulayman: grant me a kingdom that will not belong to anyone after me, *indeed You are al-Wahhāb* (38:35).

Guidance and a steady heart cannot be bought with deeds. You can only be given them. Naming Him al-Wahhāb at the end of the request is acknowledging exactly that.
