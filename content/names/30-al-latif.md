## Meaning

Two senses held together. *Luṭf* is gentleness and kindness. And *laṭīf* is fine, subtle, imperceptible, able to reach into the smallest place without being noticed.

So: the One whose kindness arrives by routes too fine to trace.

## Lessons for the believer

Yusuf uses it at the end of his story, after the pit, the slavery, the false accusation and the prison: *my Lord is Laṭīf in what He wills* (12:100). Twenty years of apparent disaster, and the word he reaches for is *subtle kindness*.

This is the name for the part of your life you cannot currently make sense of. Its claim is not that the pain was not real. It is that the arrangement was finer than you could see from inside it.
