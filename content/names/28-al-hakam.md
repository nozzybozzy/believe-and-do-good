## Meaning

The One who judges between disputants, whose ruling cannot be appealed, delayed or bought.

## Lessons for the believer

Human justice is a good thing and worth fighting for, and it will still leave a residue: the cases never brought, the evidence lost, the powerful never charged.

This name says the residue is not lost, only deferred. *Shall I seek other than Allah as judge, when it is He who has revealed to you the Book explained in detail?* (6:114). Knowing there is a final court is what makes it possible to pursue justice without being consumed by the cases you cannot win.
