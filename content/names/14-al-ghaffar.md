## Meaning

The root means to cover. A *mighfar* is the helmet a warrior wears, protection laid over the head. Forgiveness in Arabic is not the sin being argued away. It is the sin being covered so that it neither disgraces you nor harms you.

And the form is intensive and repeated: not one who forgave, but one who forgives, over and over.

## Lessons for the believer

Nuh called his people to this name with a promise attached: ask forgiveness of your Lord, He is ever *Ghaffāran*, and He will send the sky upon you in abundance, and give you wealth and children and gardens (71:10–12).

The despair that comes from returning to the same sin is answered by the form of the word itself. It is built for repetition.
