## Meaning

The King — but the Arabic carries what English loses. A human king rules what he does not own and did not make. Al-Malik owns the thing He rules, made it, sustains it, and could end it without effort.

## Lessons for the believer

The Qur'an keeps asking, on the Day of Judgement, *whose is the sovereignty today?* — and then answers its own question, because there is no one left to answer it.

Every authority you deal with is borrowed and temporary. The manager, the official, the government: each holds a scrap of delegated power for a short time and will be asked about it. Knowing who the King actually is does not make you rude to authority. It makes you unafraid of it.
