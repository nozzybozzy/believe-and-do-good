## Meaning

Awareness of the inside of things, motives, hidden causes, what a matter really is beneath how it looks. Where al-ʿAlīm is knowledge of everything, al-Khabīr is knowledge of the inward reality of it.

## Lessons for the believer

It is repeatedly paired with al-Laṭīf, and the pairing is deliberate: the One subtle enough to reach anywhere is also the One aware of what He finds there.

Luqman puts it to his son as a warning and a comfort at once: if a deed were the weight of a mustard seed, inside a rock or in the heavens or the earth, Allah will bring it forth, *indeed Allah is Laṭīf, Khabīr* (31:16).
