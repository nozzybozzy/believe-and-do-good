## Meaning

The One who brings low. The pair of al-Muʿizz, and like it, an act rather than a disposition, given to whom He wills, when He wills.

The same root produces *dhalūl*, the tame and willing mount, and the beautiful description of the believers in 5:54: *humble toward the believers, mighty against the disbelievers*.

## Lessons for the believer

There are two ways to be lowered. One is imposed. The other is chosen.

The believer chooses it, toward Allah, toward parents, toward other believers, and in doing so is protected from having it imposed. The arrogant refuse to bow to anything and are eventually bowed by something.
