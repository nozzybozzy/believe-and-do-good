## Meaning

*Raʾfah* is the tenderest form of mercy, the kind that cannot bear to see suffering. The scholars distinguish it from *raḥmah*: mercy may still allow a hard thing for a good reason, while *raʾfah* is the shrinking from harm itself.

## Lessons for the believer

The Qur'an uses it of the Prophet ﷺ as well: *there has come to you a Messenger from among yourselves; grievous to him is what you suffer, concerned over you, and to the believers kind and merciful* (9:128). The word describing Allah is applied to him.

Which sets the direction of travel. The names are not only to be believed. Some of them are to be imitated, at human scale.
