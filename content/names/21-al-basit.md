## Meaning

To spread out and extend, the way a cloth is unrolled. Used of provision, of the earth laid out, and of the chest opened up.

## Lessons for the believer

The expansion the Qur'an cares about most is not of income but of the chest: *did We not expand for you your breast?* (94:1). A person can have very little and be spacious inside, and a person can have everything and be constricted.

Both conditions come from the same pair of names, and both are temporary. The one to ask for is the expansion of the chest, because it is the one that does not depend on circumstances.
