## Meaning

The One in whose decree harm occurs. Never said alone: it is the pair of an-Nāfiʿ, the Bringer of benefit, and the tradition treats them as a single statement.

## Lessons for the believer

It is a hard name, and the reason it is in the list is important. If harm came from a source outside His authority, there would be a second power in the universe — and the Qur'an's whole case is that there is not.

The Prophet ﷺ told Ibn Abbas: if the whole nation gathered to benefit you with something, they could not benefit you except with what Allah had written for you; and if they gathered to harm you, they could not harm you except with what He had written.

That is not fatalism. It is the removal of every intermediary you might otherwise fear.
