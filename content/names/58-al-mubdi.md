## Meaning

The One who begins — who brings a thing into existence for the first time, from nothing, with no pattern to copy.

## Lessons for the believer

It is always understood with its pair, al-Muʿīd, the One who repeats. Together they make the Qur'an's central argument for resurrection: *it is He who begins creation and then repeats it, and that is easier for Him* (30:27).

*Easier* is said by way of accommodation to how people reason, since nothing is hard for Him. But the logic lands: nobody who accepts the first act should find the second one implausible.
