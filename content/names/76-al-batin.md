## Meaning

Hidden — not in the sense of absent, but of being nearer than what can be observed. The Prophet ﷺ explained it as: nothing is nearer than Him.

## Lessons for the believer

Aẓ-Ẓāhir and al-Bāṭin sit in one breath in 57:3, and the combination is the whole of the Muslim's relationship with God. He is beyond everything and closer than everything, at once.

*We are closer to him than his jugular vein* (50:16). A person who feels God is distant is not describing a fact about Him. They are describing a fact about their own attention.
