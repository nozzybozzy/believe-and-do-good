Word had gone ahead. Every morning the people of Yathrib walked out to the edge of the city and waited in the heat until it became unbearable, then went home. The day he actually arrived they had just given up and gone indoors, and it was a Jew on a rooftop who saw the party coming and shouted it across the city.

He reached **Quba**, then a settlement outside Yathrib and now inside Madinah, on Monday the 8th of Rabi' al-Awwal in the fourteenth year of prophethood — 23 September 622 CE.

He stayed four days, Monday to Thursday, and in those four days he built a mosque. It is the **first mosque built in Islam**, and the Qur'an calls it a mosque *founded on piety from the first day* (9:108).

The order of operations is worth sitting with. He had been driven out of his birthplace, had spent three nights in a cave, had ridden for days under a price on his head, and had arrived in a city he had never lived in. The first thing he did with the first four spare days he had was build a place to pray.

On the fifth day he set out for the city itself, and prayed the first Jumu'ah of Islam on the way.
