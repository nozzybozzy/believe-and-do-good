The dream that had sent fourteen hundred men to Hudaybiyah was fulfilled the following year, exactly as the treaty had specified and not a day sooner.

*Allah has certainly shown His Messenger the vision in truth: you will surely enter the Sacred Mosque, if Allah wills, in safety* (48:27). The verse had come with a qualification inside it, *if Allah wills*, and a year's delay attached.

Quraysh vacated the city for three days and went up onto the surrounding hills to watch.

There had been talk in Makkah that the fever of Madinah had weakened the Muslims. So he told his companions to uncover their right shoulders and to move briskly in the first three circuits, so that the men on the hills could see exactly what had come into their city. That brisk step, ramal, is still performed by pilgrims today.

**Bilal** climbed onto the roof of the Ka'bah and gave the adhan over Makkah.

On the third day Quraysh sent word that the time was up. He left without argument, having taken the three days agreed and not a fourth.

The restraint is the whole event. He had the numbers to stay. The terms said three days, so it was three days. Keeping a treaty that had been signed on humiliating terms, in the city that had expelled him, in front of the men who had done it, that is what made the conquest a year later look to Makkah like something other than revenge.
