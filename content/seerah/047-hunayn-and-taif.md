**Hawazin** and **Thaqif** would not live beside a Muslim Makkah. Malik ibn Awf gathered the tribes and moved against the Muslims before they could consolidate.

He went out with twelve thousand, ten thousand from Madinah and two thousand new Makkan Muslims. It was the largest force the Muslims had ever put in the field, and someone said aloud that they would not be defeated today for want of numbers.

Hawazin were archers, and they had reached the valley of **Hunayn** first and taken the high ground on both sides of a narrow defile.

The Muslim column entered in the dark before dawn and was hit from every direction at once. The front broke into the ranks behind it and the whole army came apart and ran.

He did not move. He went forward, on a white mule, calling out who he was into a fleeing army:

> *I am the Prophet, this is no lie; I am the son of Abdul Muttalib.*

Al-Abbas, who had a powerful voice, was told to call the Ansar and the men of the tree. They came back, first in tens, then in hundreds, and the battle turned completely. Hawazin broke and fled.

The Qur'an's comment on this is one of the most direct in the Book: *and on the day of Hunayn, when your great numbers pleased you but availed you nothing, and the earth in its vastness closed in on you, and you turned back in flight* (9:25).

They had fought Badr at 313 and won, and Hunayn at 12,000 and nearly lost. The lesson arrived precisely at the point of greatest strength.

The survivors fortified themselves in **Ta'if**, the same town that had stoned him out twenty years before. He besieged it for about two weeks, and then, told that they had over a year of supplies, lifted the siege and withdrew. Asked to curse them, he prayed for them instead. They came to Madinah as Muslims the following year.

The spoils from Hunayn were enormous, and he distributed them heavily to the new Makkan Muslims. The Ansar felt it, and said so among themselves. He gathered them and asked: did I not find you astray and Allah guided you through me, divided and Allah united you? Are you not content that the people go home with sheep and camels, while you go home with the Messenger of Allah? They wept, and said they were content with Allah and His Messenger as their share.
