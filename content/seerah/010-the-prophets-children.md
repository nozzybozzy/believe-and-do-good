Khadijah bore him al-Qasim, Zaynab, Ruqayyah, Umm Kulthum, Fatimah and Abdullah. Only Ibrahim, much later in Madinah, was born to another, Maria.

The sons all died as small children. Ibrahim died as an infant too, and the Prophet ﷺ wept openly and said the words that have steadied Muslim parents ever since: the eye sheds tears and the heart grieves, and we say only what our Lord is pleased with.

Of the daughters, three, Zaynab, Ruqayyah and Umm Kulthum, died before him. Fatimah outlived him by six months.

In a lineage-obsessed society, a man with no surviving son was *abtar*: cut off, a line that ends. When al-Qasim died, one of the Prophet's enemies used exactly that word about him in public, you can stop worrying about this man, his name dies with him.

The answer was Surah Al-Kawthar: We have given you al-Kawthar. Three verses. It does not deny the loss. It reframes the accounting entirely, abundance is given, the command is to pray and sacrifice, and *the one cut off is the one who hates you.* The insult is picked up and handed back.

Fourteen centuries later, the name of the man who said it is remembered only because of the verse that answered him, and the name of the Prophet ﷺ is said aloud somewhere on earth in every minute of every day.
