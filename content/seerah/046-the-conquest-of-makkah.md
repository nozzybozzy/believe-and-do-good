The treaty had allowed tribes to ally with either side. **Khuza'ah** allied with the Muslims; **Banu Bakr** with Quraysh. Old feuds revived, Banu Bakr attacked Khuza'ah with Quraysh supplying them, and the killing continued into the sanctuary itself.

That broke the treaty, and Quraysh knew it. Abu Sufyan travelled to Madinah to try to restore it and found no one who would speak for him, including his own daughter, who pulled the Prophet's bedding out from under him because he was an idolater and it was the Messenger of Allah's.

He marched on the 10th of Ramadan, 8 AH, with **ten thousand**. Every precaution was taken to make the move invisible until it was too late to resist.

The army camped at Mar az-Zahran and lit ten thousand fires. Abu Sufyan came out to see what was happening, met **al-Abbas**, and was brought to the Prophet ﷺ. That night he accepted Islam. And he was given something to carry back: whoever enters the house of Abu Sufyan is safe, whoever closes his own door is safe, whoever enters the Sacred Mosque is safe.

Makkah surrendered without a battle.

He entered not as a conqueror. Onlookers describe him on his camel with his head bowed so low in humility that his beard nearly touched the saddle, reciting Surah Al-Fath.

Then the Ka'bah. There were **360 idols** in and around it, and he went round striking them down, reciting: *Truth has come and falsehood has vanished; falsehood was bound to vanish* (17:81). The images inside were erased. Bilal — bought out of slavery, once staked on the burning sand of this city by men now standing in the crowd — climbed onto the roof of the Ka'bah and called the adhan.

Then the people of Makkah, gathered and waiting, and the question he put to them: *what do you think I am going to do with you?*

They said: a noble brother and the son of a noble brother.

Twenty-one years of boycott, torture, exile and war, and the whole of it fell to a single sentence — the same words Yusuf gave his brothers: **no blame upon you this day; go, you are free.**

A handful of individuals were excepted for specific crimes, and most of even those were pardoned when they came. **Hind**, who had chewed Hamzah's liver, accepted Islam. **Wahshi**, who had killed him, accepted Islam.
