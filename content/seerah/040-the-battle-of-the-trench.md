This was the attempt at extermination. Abu Sufyan raised four thousand from Makkah alone; **Ghatafan** and **Banu Sulaym** joined from the east; and Banu an-Nadir, now in exile at Khaybar, worked the tribes. Ten thousand men marched on a city of a few thousand. The Qur'an calls them **al-Ahzab**, the Confederates.

He consulted, as always. **Salman al-Farisi** — a Persian, a former slave, a man who had gone looking for the truth across three religions and several countries before finding it in Madinah — said that in Persia, when they faced a siege, they dug a trench.

Nobody in Arabia fought this way. He gave the order immediately. Every ten men took forty yards, and he dug with them. They worked hungry, in cold, tying stones to their stomachs — and he tied two.

When the Confederates arrived they found a trench across the exposed northern approach and could not cross it. Cavalry, their whole advantage, was useless. So they settled in to starve the city out.

Then the news that Banu Qurayzah, inside the city, had gone over to the enemy — which meant the defenders now had a front behind them where their own women and children were. *When they came at you from above you and from below you, and when eyes shifted and hearts reached the throats* (33:10). The hypocrites said the promises of Allah and His Messenger had been nothing but delusion.

And in the same trench, the same event read the other way: *When the believers saw the Confederates, they said: this is what Allah and His Messenger promised us, and Allah and His Messenger spoke the truth. It only increased them in faith and submission* (33:22).

Twenty-seven days. Then two things ended it. **Nu'aym ibn Mas'ud** of Ghatafan accepted Islam without his people knowing, and was sent back to work suspicion between Quraysh, Ghatafan and Qurayzah — which he did so effectively that none of them would move without hostages from the others. And then a violent winter storm tore through the camp at night, ripping out tents and putting out fires, and the alliance simply left.

*We sent against them a wind and forces you did not see* (33:9).

Madinah was never attacked again in his lifetime. From this point the initiative passed permanently to the Muslims.
