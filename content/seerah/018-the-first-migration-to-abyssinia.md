By the middle of the fifth year of prophethood there was nothing left to absorb. He gave permission to leave.

A small group went — twelve men and four women — among them **Uthman ibn Affan** and his wife **Ruqayyah**, the Prophet's own daughter.

The destination was chosen deliberately. Abyssinia was Christian, and its king, the Negus, had a reputation: in his land, no one was wronged. That was the entire basis of the decision. Not shared creed — shared justice. A community of monotheists fled a city full of the Sacred House and took shelter with a Christian king, because the question that mattered was whether a ruler would deal fairly with people who had no power.

They stayed a few months. Then word reached them that Quraysh had accepted Islam, and they came home — to find it false, and the situation worse than when they had left.
